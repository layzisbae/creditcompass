  • Campus and district licenses (coming soon): caseload dashboards and policy-change alerts for counseling teams.

Advising checklist

  • Send official AP scores (College Board) and IB results (IBO); send dual-credit transcripts from the college, not the high school.
  • Texas publics: Texas Core courses transfer as core (Educ. Code §61.822); AP minimums above 3 need an evidence-based exception, and IB Diploma holders get at least 24 hrs (§51.968).
  • Selective schools often give placement only, or limit exam credit. Expect 8 semesters unless the school says otherwise.
  • Pre-med and nursing: check whether target programs accept AP for prerequisites.

Sources & method

Where every number comes from, and how to read it.

1

Match each score to a policy

Each college's official credit chart sets a minimum score and what you get (e.g. AP Calculus BC 4 → Calculus I + II). Placement counts as 0 hours. Dual credit uses Texas Common Course Numbers and each college's transfer rules.

2

Sort credit by where it lands

Major requirements first, then core / gen-ed areas (each area capped), then free electives up to the major's elective room. Anything beyond is awarded but may not shorten the degree. College limits on exam credit are applied first.

3

Convert to dollars & semesters

Applicable hours × cost per credit hour = dollars. Cost per hour = annual tuition & fees ÷ 30 hours. Hours ÷ 15, rounded down = full semesters saved.

The formulas

applicable_hours = major + core + min(elective, elective_cap[major])
cost_per_hour   = annual_tuition_and_fees[college] ÷ 30
dollars_saved   = applicable_hours × cost_per_hour
semesters_saved = floor(applicable_hours ÷ 15)
credits_lost    = hours not earned (score too low, not accepted, duplicate, over a cap)
  • Tuition basis: IPEDS 2024–25 tuition & required fees from College Navigator (NCES). Texas publics use in-state; Georgia Tech, Michigan and Oklahoma use out-of-state, since that's what a Texan pays. Housing, books and aid aren't included.
  • Upper bound: need-based aid lowers what many families pay, especially at private schools, so real dollar savings are often smaller.
  • Flat tuition & 8 semesters: most private and elite schools charge a flat rate and expect 8 semesters, so credit mainly buys flexibility (a minor, double major, research) unless you formally graduate early. Schools that don't let exam credit shorten the degree show $0 with a flexibility note.
  • Why round down? A partial semester rarely lets you graduate earlier. You can switch to nearest in the settings.
  • Unit conversions: quarter units, course units and "credits" are converted to semester hours approximately (e.g. Penn 1 c.u. ≈ 4 hrs).

Texas law

  • Texas Education Code §51.968: Texas public universities may not require more than a 3 on an AP exam for lower-division credit unless the chief academic officer finds, based on evidence, that a higher score is needed. Students who complete the IB Diploma must get at least 24 semester hours (fewer only for exam scores below 4). The 24-hour IB Diploma minimum isn't modeled here; IB is scored subject by subject. Read §51.968
  • Texas Education Code §61.822: courses that complete the Texas Core Curriculum at one Texas public institution transfer as core to the others. Read §61.822
